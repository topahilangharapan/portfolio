thesis

  • Bridging the Gap Between the Mano Register-Transfer Model and FPGA Realization Through a Synthesisable VHDL-93 Architecture undergraduate thesis, Universitas Indonesia Fasilkom — 2026

A complete synthesisable VHDL-93 implementation of the Mano register-transfer model, running at 58.648 MHz on a Xilinx Spartan-3AN FPGA (1,675 LUTs, 14% utilisation; 638 flip-flops, 5% utilisation). The thesis conducts a systematic gap analysis of the Mano model's undefined engineering constraints — register organisation, instruction encoding, control mechanism, memory partitioning, and I/O interface — and maps each to a concrete VHDL-93 design decision, producing a verified 8-bit datapath implementing the full microoperation sequence with board-level testing.
