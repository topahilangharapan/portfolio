A complete synthesisable VHDL-93 implementation of the Mano register-transfer model, running at 58.648 MHz on a Xilinx Spartan-3AN FPGA (1,675 LUTs at 14% utilisation and 638 flip-flops at 5% utilisation). The thesis identifies five unspecified engineering gaps in the Mano model (register organisation, instruction encoding, control mechanism, memory partitioning, and I/O interface) and resolves each with an original design decision, including a 32-bit control word and a three-bus datapath enabling single-cycle register-to-register operations.

Verified with 25 self-checking testbenches across five hierarchy tiers under ISim and GHDL, plus board-level testing and LCD I/O integration. Found and fixed a board-only defect every testbench had missed: a race between a memory address cursor and its write strobe, closed with two regression tests.
